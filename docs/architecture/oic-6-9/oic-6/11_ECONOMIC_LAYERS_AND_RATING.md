# OIC-6 v1.0 — economic layers, price books and rating

**Owner:** OIC accounting domain; OIC runtime supplies attempts. Existing `OicUpstreamPricingEvidence` is discovery metadata, never an invoice or a complete price book. All proposed tables are OIC-owned UUID records with application/tenant scope where attributable. Published revisions are immutable; draft revisions may be edited until publication. Every rating result pins effective revision and native evidence. Operators use canonical X1 controls; no customer-facing OIC portal is implied.

## Three disjoint layers

1. **Provider economics:** `ProviderPriceBookRevision` owns provider connection/service/model selector, currency, effective UTC half-open interval, billing dimension rates, tier rules and source/evidence quality. `ProviderCostAssessment` is a derived immutable record per attempt. Status is `INVOICE_AUTHORITATIVE`, `PROVIDER_REPORTED`, `DERIVED_FROM_AUTHORITATIVE_USAGE`, `ESTIMATED` or `UNKNOWN`; invoice status only after scoped invoice reconciliation, never because catalog metadata exists.
2. **Internal economics:** `OiuPolicyRevision` maps normalized resource dimensions to OIU; `OiuAssessment` records exact inputs and output. `InternalCostAssessment` optionally attributes retrieval, vector search, tools, storage, evaluation work and internal compute with its own source/status. OIU is neither cost nor price; internal cost v1 may remain unavailable.
3. **Customer commercial economics:** `CustomerPriceBookRevision` is separate from provider price, with a bounded strategy enum (`OIU_RATE`, `RESOURCE_RATE`, `REQUEST`, `TASK`, `FEATURE`, `TIERED`, `INCLUDED_PLUS_OVERAGE`, `FIXED_PLUS_VARIABLE`) and typed parameter rows. `RatedUsageEvent` is an immutable OIC charge *calculation/evidence* linked to usage event, plan/contract revision, native currency and rating trace. OIC is authoritative for its rated-usage calculation, **not** legally issued invoices, tax, payment or receivable; a future Finance system owns those. External charging stays feature-disabled pending owner decision.

## Price resolution and currency

Provider price match key: `(provider connection or explicit provider, upstream service/model, dimension, region/service class if contractual, billing mode, effective time)`; most-specific explicit contract wins, then published standard book. A negotiated enterprise rate, volume tier, commitment, batch or cache discount is an explicit revisioned rule, never inferred from published retail. More than one equally specific active rule is publication-time conflict and rating-time `PRICE_AMBIGUOUS`; missing rule yields `UNKNOWN`, never zero. Publish transaction rejects overlapping intervals for same match key and precedence. Effective time is provider event occurrence, with documented late-report policy. Capture selected rate row, quantity, unit, decimal amount and evidence source per dimension. Supports input/output/cached/cache-write tokens, explicitly billed reasoning units, image class, audio/video duration, embeddings, rerank, tool, storage, batch, priority, request and provider-specific typed dimensions. Unrecognized dimensions are preserved as raw usage and remain unrated.

Provider cost and customer price each retain native amount/currency; cross-currency reporting uses `FxRateEvidence` with source, rate timestamp, base/quote and conversion-policy revision. Missing FX is `UNKNOWN_CONVERSION`, not parity. Currency minor-unit rounding happens once at final rated-event total using currency metadata, with line-level exact decimals retained. No taxes are computed in OIC. Discounts/credits/trials are explicit separate rated adjustments referencing original event, not overwritten amounts.

Customer rating precedence after authorization: immutable effective plan assignment → most-specific published contract override (tenant, then application) → time-bound approved promotion/trial → customer price-book revision → included allowance accounting → overage policy. Conflicting equal-specificity overrides reject publication; a tenant override cannot escape application scope. No published customer price or authorization means `NOT_RATED`, not free. Commercial plan patterns `FREE|TRIAL|PREPAID|POSTPAID|SUBSCRIPTION|ENTERPRISE_CUSTOM` are assignment metadata; activation, funding and invoicing remain separate. Overage defaults denied. Plan revision defines model tier/feature entitlements, output/concurrency ceilings, included OIU/requests, evaluation/learning allowances, hard/soft quotas and optional commercial schedule. A plan snapshot is pinned at admission; a mid-call plan change affects next admission only.

**Normative rating registry:** strategies are typed, versioned handlers with fixed input/output contracts: `OIU_RATE` (rated OIU × price/OIU), `RESOURCE_RATE` (typed quantity × price/unit), `REQUEST` (one qualified event × fixed amount), `TASK` (one defined completed task × amount), `FEATURE` (authorized feature event × amount), `TIERED` (ordered quantity bands), `INCLUDED_PLUS_OVERAGE` (consume allowance then price only excess), `FIXED_PLUS_VARIABLE` (period charge reference plus variable event). A published customer book selects exactly one top-level strategy revision and typed dimensions; handlers cannot execute arbitrary expressions. The rating trace lists precedence winner, inclusion/discount/credit applications and every decimal intermediate. Free/trial does **not** suppress raw usage, provider expense, OIU or quota debit; it produces an explicit zero-rated customer event with `FREE_PLAN|TRIAL_CREDIT` reason and source policy, only if an active published rule authorizes it. Credits/reversals are new signed commercial adjustment rows pointing to original rated event; no mutation or duplicate credit under replay. A plan revision effective mid-period applies to new admissions from its effective instant; existing reservation, included allowance bucket and rating remain pinned to old revision for that invocation. A new assignment may prorate allowance only if its published rule defines exact proration; otherwise no inferred rollover/proration.

## OIU exactness and ledger choice

One OIU = one internal normalized consumption unit by convention, not a fixed token or monetary quantity. `OiuPolicyRevision` declares dimension unit, nonnegative rational weight, included/excluded dimensions, cache multiplier, modality factor, minimum posting increment and missing-dimension rule. Compute in decimal at scale >=12, reject nonfinite/negative/overflow, sum exact dimension products, round once half-even to six decimal places; zero is allowed only from complete known zero input under policy. A missing required dimension gives `PARTIAL` or `UNKNOWN` and no authoritative OIU posting; optional dimensions are explicitly excluded by policy, not assumed zero. Hidden reasoning resources count only when provider reports a contractual billable dimension. Tool/retrieval/internal compute are separate dimensions when measured; no arbitrary token-equivalence hidden in adapter code. Pin policy digest, raw units and normalized units for replay. Historical re-rating is a new analytical assessment, never a mutation.

Use **three semantic append-only journals**, sharing immutable source IDs but distinct types and controls: usage/OIU postings, provider/internal cost assessments and adjustments, customer rated events and commercial adjustments. A single giant typed ledger would blur monetary authority and make scope/retention controls harder. Each journal has unique `(sourceType, sourceId, policyRevisionId, assessmentKind)` for original assessment and explicit revisioned correction chain. No negative raw usage; corrections use signed adjustment with reason and approval. Aggregate profitability is `rated customer amount - provider cost - evidenced internal cost` only when currency conversion and all component statuses permit; otherwise show partial/estimated with coverage, never audited profit. Group by app/tenant/model/binding/profile/time through immutable IDs. OIC-7 consumes cost/OIU/latency per case with evidence levels, deriving cost per *measured successful* case and Pareto frontiers without rewriting accounting.

**Property-test-ready invariants:** original journal row count is monotonic; one source+revision+kind yields at most one original assessment; adjustments/reversals reference an existing same-scope row and never delete it; normalized units and OIU are nonnegative for originals; `reserved + consumed` projection equals replayed posting groups per bucket at each committed sequence; a transfer/settlement changes held and consumed exactly once, and no successful admission leaves `held + consumed > hardLimit` at commit (provider overrun is explicit debt after dispatch); all money retains native currency and a balanced correction chain within that currency; no implicit FX arithmetic; applying the same receipt/credit/adjustment idempotency key twice yields identical journal/projection; recomputing from pinned inputs/revisions reproduces the same six-decimal OIU and rated-event amount. Rebuildable read models are derived from immutable journal sequence and last-applied pointer; a rebuild never changes original rows or historical policy. If source evidence is missing/redacted, a projection marks availability rather than manufacturing a value.

**Indexes/query:** `(applicationId, tenantId, occurredAt, id)`, source attempt unique key, price-book match/effective interval, `(planAssignment scope, effectiveFrom)`, rating original/adjustment chain; bounded cursor reads and partitioning only after volume evidence. **Audit:** publish, override, reconcile and export commands with actor/revision/diff hash; derived records retain producer/version. **Retention:** policy-governed economic evidence and source rates remain interpretable after content redaction. **Tests:** price interval conflict, tier boundaries, missing dimensions, six-decimal rounding, overflow, FX unavailable, replay, plan mid-call, correction chain, mixed-currency reporting. **Rollout:** shadow provider rating and OIU first; customer rating read-only/dark until activation decision.
