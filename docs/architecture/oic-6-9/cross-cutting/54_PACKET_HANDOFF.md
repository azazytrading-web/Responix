## First implementation return point: OIC-6.1 Raw evidence

**Scope:** capture and reconcile source-backed provider attempt and raw usage evidence with provenance, digest, and scope. It establishes evidence only; it makes no monetary claim and excludes OIU rating, customer rating, entitlement, quota, and enforcement.

**Prerequisites:** OIC-6 through OIC-9 v1.0 architecture lock; OIC-5/X1 runtime attempt and receipt identities verified against the repository; scoped trusted runtime capture/read path and secret-safe evidence allowlist agreed within the locked contract. No earlier OIC-6 packet is required.

**Acceptance gate:** duplicate/replay, unknown usage, tenant/application scope, and secret-safety checks pass; shadow capture reconciles to source traces with complete labeled provenance; unknown values remain explicitly unknown; no charge or quota behavior is claimed. The owner accepts evidence coverage before OIC-6.2 begins. Implementation is not authorized by this architecture record alone.

## Owner-approved Packet 7.4 acceptance amendment (2026-10-15)

The owner-approved [`Packet 7.4 acceptance amendment`](../oic-7/28_PACKET_7_4_ACCEPTANCE_AMENDMENT_2026-10-15.md) clarifies the 7.4 row above: real-human acceptance requires two independent, eligible, non-conflicted reviews of the same blinded item(s), immutable attributable submissions, durable audit evidence, and canonical inter-rater computation. The observed human result may be agreement or disagreement; natural disagreement is not an acceptance prerequisite. Disagreement computation, preservation, audit, projection, and adjudication behavior remain required implementation and deterministic-test coverage. This amendment changes only the acceptance interpretation for Packet 7.4 and preserves the historical contract and all identity, calibration, conflict, blindness, immutability, audit, application-isolation, and protected-data guarantees.
